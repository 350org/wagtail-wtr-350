When "Manual country override" is set on the integration (QA/local testing of a specific country), it's used directly and synchronously. Otherwise, fetch Cloudflare's built-in /cdn-cgi/trace edge endpoint (served directly by Cloudflare, never reaches Django, so this never touches the page's own edge cache or origin) and parse its "loc=XX" line for the visitor's country.
