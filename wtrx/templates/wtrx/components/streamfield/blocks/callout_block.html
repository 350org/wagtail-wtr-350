Text sits directly on top (no card/box) on the OPPOSITE side from the image — each line gets a tight white "highlighter" background via .wtr-callout-highlight (box-decoration-break: clone, see main.css) rather than one padded rectangle behind the whole paragraph. Spill: the text overlay is a SIBLING of the image (positioned relative to the outer, full-width wrapper, not clipped by the image's own overflow-hidden), so it's meant to spill horizontally past the image's edge into the empty ~20% gutter on the side opposite the image. It must NOT spill vertically though — bottom-4/8 insets it up from the bottom edge (not translated past it), and since the image is the only normal- flow content, the outer wrapper's height equals the image's height, so "bottom" here is anchored to the image's own bottom edge. flex + items-end/items-start (not text-align + margin-left/right:auto) for the alignment — a max-width-only block's width stays "auto" under the hood, which forces any auto margin to resolve to 0 before max-width clamps it (CSS2.1 10.3.3's over-constrained-auto-margins rule). Flexbox's own alignment algorithm doesn't have that gotcha. {% endcomment %}
